# OpenDroneMap

*July 31st 2026*

![OpenDroneMap WebODM split](/images/opendronemap-webodm.jpg)

As of 2026, there's a lot of outdated information about OpenDroneMap and its relationship with WebODM. This page provides some up-to-date information:

 * As of April 6th 2026, the projects have split. WebODM **was** part of OpenDroneMap, but that is no longer the case. You can read the [announcement](/blog/announcement) for details.
 * WebODM installers are available to download **for free** from our [download page](/download) at [webodm.org](https://webodm.org).
 * WebODM **was** an interface to OpenDroneMap (ODM), but that is no longer the case. Today WebODM uses [ODX](https://github.com/WebODM/ODX), a state of the art, up-to-date photogrammetry engine forked from and faster than ODM, maintained by former core and lead contributors of ODM that is entirely separate from OpenDroneMap.
 * OpenDroneMap sells installers for a fork of WebODM, called "OpenDroneMap Desktop", which is **not** WebODM and should not be confused with WebODM. 
 * Some references about WebODM on the OpenDroneMap website are outdated or inaccurate and should be disregarded.
